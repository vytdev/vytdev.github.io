# A Key to Fighting Diabetes?

What if something as ordinary as coffee is secretly a key to fight diabetes,
hiding in plain sight? Researchers at the Kunming Institute of Botany, Chinese
Academy of Sciences in China have made a discovery that roasted coffee beans
contain previously unknown sugar-inhibiting compounds, compounds needed to
reverse diabetes.

The compound works by targeting an enzyme responsible for breaking down
carbohydrates into sugar, which could help lessen blood sugar spikes. In
laboratory tests, the molecules were even more potent than a common
anti-diabetic drug. Though they haven't conducted a test on humans yet.

While coffee on itself is not a cure to diabetes, this discovery gave us
insights about the existence of these previously unknown compounds, which
experts may eventually be able to utilize in the future. It suggests that some
of the solutions we have been searching for have always been there all along.
