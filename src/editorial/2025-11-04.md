# Science Has Just Identified Six Tube-nosed Bats

Somewhere beneath the towering forest trees lie creatures we've never seen
before. Researchers from Lawrence University in the United States have
officially recognized 6 new tube-nosed bat species from the Philippines, named
after the unique shape of their nostrils.

The bats are as follows: Alvarez’s tube-nosed bat, Balete’s tube-nosed bat,
Hilong-Hilong tube-nosed bat, Luzon tube-nosed bat, Philippine tube-nosed bat,
and Mindoro tube-nosed bat. These bats were found from different parts of the
Philippines. Though they all have varying traits, they share one distinct
thing in common &mdash; their unique tube-shaped nostrils.

"These bats are notoriously elusive, so the tube-nosed bat collection this
study examined was cobbled together over many years, expeditions, and
memorable experiences &mdash; one bat at a time", said Jodi Sedlock, study
co-author.

The researchers also wrote that all six bats were captured within forests and
protected areas that are threatened by agriculture and mining. Their habitats
reportedly had lost 4%+ of their forest cover over the past few decades.
There are also 58 large-scale mines currently operating in the Philippines,
the researchers say.

It's worth noting that while discovering these new-to-science creatures
leaves us in awe, we've been unknowingly posing a threat to their environment
&mdash; and by extension, to their species. We should take more responsibility
and make the right choices, because money won't be able to replace their lives.
