# What Global Unity Can Achieve

There was a time when all countries in the world united to prevent a
global catastrophe &mdash; and the World Meteorological Organization (WMO)
Ozone Bulletin shows that that was really effective.

According to the WMO Ozone Bulletin, the ozone hole in 2024 is smaller
compared to previous years. The ozone hole is a giant, invisible thinning
in the atmosphere discovered in 1980s. It appeared after our large-scale
use of substances such as CFCs &mdash; used in old refrigerators and air
conditioners.

The ozone hole became a threat as it allowed more ultraviolet (UV)
radiation to reach the Earth's surface. Excessive UV radiation is harmful
because it can cause skin cancers, cataracts, and weaken our immune
system. It also disrupts the environment by damaging plants and animals.

After the scientists discovered the problem, the world worked together
to stop it from getting worse. In 1987, the Montreal Protocol was
established to phase out the use of such substances. It was ratified
by every country in the globe and remains one of the few treaties to
achieve universal agreement.

"Forty years ago, nations came together to take the first step in
protecting the ozone layer &mdash; guided by science, united in action", said
United Nations Secretary-General António Guterres. The WMO continues to
actively monitor the Earth's atmosphere.

It is an evidence that even though we have our differences, we can still
unite and overcome a common threat.
